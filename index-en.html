Turn One Idea into a Complete Project in Five Courses

Research a topic, shape a story, design characters, produce moving images and sound, and present the result on your own website. Each exercise adds another piece to your final project.

2026.10.11—2027.01.10 13 Saturdays + 2 Sundays · In person + online Five courses · One complete project
Knowing how to use AI is only the beginning. The goal is to become a versatile creator who can bring ideas to life.

Writing, images, music, and video are all tools for expressing ideas. Across five courses, you will learn to ask questions, choose a direction, organize your creative work, and build each class exercise into a final project that truly extends your creativity with AI.

01 / OBJECTIVE

What Will You Learn?

This bootcamp takes you through a complete creative process: research a topic, tell a story, establish a visual style, produce video and sound, and publish your work.

Start with an idea and create work that is truly your own.

You will practice asking questions, evaluating sources, structuring a story, and bringing visuals and sound together. You will also judge whether AI-generated material is accurate and appropriate. Tools can help you move faster, but the theme, aesthetic, and final decisions remain yours.

You will practice: Research × Storytelling × Audiovisual Creation × Interactive Publishing
  1. 01Find a meaningful topicSearch for and analyze sources, assess their reliability, and turn your findings into a clear creative direction.
  2. 02Turn a story into visualsDesign characters, settings, props, and a visual style from your written concept so the work stays consistent across media.
  3. 03Bring image and sound togetherExplore storyboarding, video generation, voice work, music, and sound effects so audiences can see and hear your story clearly.
  4. 04Use AI while keeping your judgmentBreak down tasks, choose tools, check facts, revise results, and document sources and how you used AI.
  5. 05Organize and present your workBring your process and final results into a personal website or portfolio, and explain your project clearly to others.
  6. 06Become a versatile creatorConnect writing, imagery, music, video, and web skills, using AI to develop your idea step by step into a complete work.
  7. 07Create 3D work in Blender with AIUse AI to guide Blender modeling, scene building, and animation, then apply your 3D work to promotional videos, games, and short creative pieces.
