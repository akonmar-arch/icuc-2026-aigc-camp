Sound becomes ink.让声音成为水墨。

Try it in this order建议按这个顺序体验

  1. Press PlayStart the built-in gentle rhythm if no file is loaded.点击播放；没有音频文件时可先听内置的轻柔节奏。
  2. Choose local MP3Load a local recording and compare quiet and strong passages.选一段本地录音，比较安静段与强拍段的画面。
  3. Use your voiceAllow the microphone only if you want to sing, hum or clap.想用歌声、哼唱或拍手时，再主动授权麦克风。
  4. Draw on the canvasDrag to leave ink traces; scroll to shift the colour tone.拖动留下墨迹；滚轮切换色调。

Microphone and camera access require your permission and HTTPS or localhost. If a browser blocks permission inside the frame, open the full page above.麦克风和摄像头须由你授权，且需要 HTTPS 或 localhost。若浏览器拦截嵌入页权限，请打开完整页面。

Ink swells / 墨环起伏

Bass energy changes the ring's size and weight. / 低频能量改变墨环的大小与粗细。

STRONG ONSETS / 强拍

Ink disperses / 墨点泼散

Detected hits release droplets that spread and fade. / 检测到强拍时，墨点向外飞散并逐渐淡去。

GESTURE / 动作

You leave a trace / 动作留下痕迹
